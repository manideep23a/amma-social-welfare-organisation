WHO WE ARE

Rooted in people.

AMMA Social Welfare Organisation is a community-focused organisation dedicated to social welfare and inclusive development.


Through dedicated community service, welfare initiatives and outreach, AMMA Social Welfare Organisation works towards creating a stronger, caring and inclusive community.


Our focus is on helping people in need, supporting communities, encouraging social responsibility and recognising people who contribute to society.
